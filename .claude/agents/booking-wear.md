## Rules (product decisions, pricing-spec.md "Source of truth")

1. **Fixed-term, post-paid rent.** A borrower books a term (`plannedDays`, 1–365). Nothing is charged at booking: each pair's deposit and its rent estimate are locked (`lockDeposit`, then `replan` → payments' `lockRent`), and the actual rent is charged only when the item closes (`closeItem` → payments' `collectRent`, same transaction). Rentals are not open-ended. (Product decision 6, 2026-10-11; replaces rent paid upfront.)
2. **Wear is committed at booking.** `Shoe.committedWear` = settled `wear` + the planned wear of every open booking on the pair. A new pair booked alone for 90 days shows 90/365 the moment it is booked. Listings show it, and the next borrower is priced from it.
3. **Day 1 is the day after delivery.** The delivery day is free. Both the first and the last day count.
4. **Early return needs notice.** The borrower picks a new last day E with today + `earlyReturnNoticeDays` (10) ≤ E < `termEndDay` (`scheduleEarlyReturn`). The term is shortened to E at once (`termEndDay = E`, `plannedDays = E − firstDay + 1`, `earlyReturnAt` set), so the rent lock for the unused days is released immediately; the borrower keeps the pair until E. Ending at once (`endRental` by the borrower) is allowed only on the term's last day, or within `freeReturnHours` of delivery (wrong size). Otherwise it is refused with the earliest day they can pick.
5. **Term end.** The daily job (`runDailyRentals`, `/api/cron/rentals`) ends every held pair on `termEndDay + 1` with `lastDay = termEndDay` and books the collection or handoff. Waiting for the driver is free.
6. **Extension** locks the extra estimate upfront, on or before `termEndDay`, and refused once someone has reserved the pair next or when the borrower's available tokens would go below zero (nothing changes).
7. **Lifespan.** A pair with `committedWear` < L can be booked for any term. Past L, wear keeps counting (1/n per day) and each wear-day is charged at the end-of-life price P(L) until the pair is returned, even past R (`chargePastLife`, on the shoe's settings snapshot): pass `!params.pastLife` as `capAtLife` to `wearEnd` / `chainWear`, and price with `incomeBetween`. Example: 364/365 booked for 20 days pays 1 curve day + 19 days at P(L) and commits wear 384. Once committed wear reaches L, `quoteBundle` refuses the pair ("at end of life"), for delivery and for reservations alike; it is retired when it comes back.
8. **1/n split per borrower (unlimited pairs).** Each day a borrower holds n pairs, each gains 1/n wear-day. Borrowers can hold any number of pairs they want without artificial limit; wear is split evenly across all held pairs. Changes are settled to actual wear: ending one pair of a bundle early raises the others' wear and rent (2 × 90 days = 45 each; A cut to day 30 → A 15, B 75).
9. **Reservations and offers (anytime forward booking, product decision 10).** A held pair can be reserved next from its holder's day 1 (Browse shows every held pair nobody has reserved; there is no release-window gate), for a term starting after the holder's `termEndDay` (+1 day for the handoff), priced at the pair's projected wear then. If a change moves a reservation's planned first day earlier (the holder's early-return notice or wrong-size return) or raises its estimate, `replan` makes an offer (`offerFirstDay`, `offerCharge`, `offerWearStart`, `offerDueAt` = now + `offerResponseHours`, 24 h) and tells the borrower in the app and by email: the new start date, the new price and why. Nothing changes until they answer; the holder keeps the pair meanwhile. Accept (`acceptOffer`): `plannedFirstDay` / `plannedCharge` / `plannedWearStart` take the offered values, the rent lock follows (refused, nothing changed, if available tokens go below zero), and the pair is handed over after the holder's last day (`handOffIfWaiting`). Decline, wanting the original dates, or expiry (`rejectOffer` / `expireOffers`): `cancelReservation` cancels the reservation, unlocks its deposit and rent estimate, and notifies the borrower (app + email: the pair came back early, the reservation is cancelled and released). With `holdPair`, the holder's item gets `holdForNext`: when the holding ends (or now, if it already has) the pair is **not** collected; it stays with the holder, free (their `lastDay` is fixed: no rent, no wear), and they are told to keep it safe. It stays in Browse; the next booking is priced from its real wear and handed over from the holder straight away (`handOffIfWaiting`). Never book a COLLECT for a `holdForNext` pair (only end of life overrides it). A lower estimate is unlocked automatically. (Product owner, 2026-10-11, overriding the pasted spec that said to collect.)

## Must deliver

1. **Wear ledger (pure, decimal.js):**
   - Calendar days in Asia/Colombo: `colomboDay`, `dayToDate` / `dateToDay` / `dayNumber`, `firstDayAfterDelivery` (D + 1), `receiverFirstDay` (max(D, giver's last day) + 1), `plannedLastDay`.
   - `wearAdded(holdings, itemId, through?)`: phase-based sum of 1/n per day across the borrower's holdings; a holding whose last day is before its first adds nothing. `wearEnd` caps at L. `chainWear(settledWear, added[], L)` chains a pair's open items.
   - `runLedger` / `estimate` / `buildInvoice` (`accrual.ts`): the reference daily accrual. It must agree with `wearAdded` day for day.
2. **Planning (`plan.ts`):** `planItems` (pure) gives every open item a planned holding (held: `firstDay` → `lastDay` or `termEndDay`; awaiting delivery: tomorrow → + `plannedDays` − 1; reserved next: max(tomorrow, holder's planned last day + 2) → + `plannedDays` − 1), its wear from the ledger and its rent from the pricing engine (`incomeBetween`, `chargedAmounts`). `replan(tx, seed, now, { actorId })` loads every borrower and pair it touches (`loadPlanItems`), stores `plannedFirstDay`, `plannedWearStart/End`, `plannedCharge` and `committedWear`, and moves each rent lock with payments' `lockRent` (target = estimate − anything already charged) or makes an offer. Call it after anything that changes a plan: checkout, delivery, early-return notice, extension, term end, offer accepted/rejected/expired, reservation cancelled, lost pair, close.
3. **Checkout (`checkout.ts`):** `quoteBundle` plans the new pairs with `planItems` alongside the borrower's bookings and each pair's queue (so the quote equals what is charged) and reports per pair the start and end wear, expected days and rent, plus `required` (Σ V + rent estimate), `available` and `shortfall`. `checkout` creates the items, locks each pair's deposit (`lockDeposit`), runs `replan` (locks the rent estimates, commits the wear), stores `Rental.estimatedRent`, and rolls back if available tokens go below zero; it requests the deliver trip, or the handoff at once if the holder already ended and is waiting.
4. **Release discount** (pricing-spec §8): stored on the item at checkout, fixed for the rental. `Shoe.releaseDay` = the holder's `termEndDay`; set on delivery, moved by extension and by early-return notice.
5. **Lifecycle (`lifecycle.ts`):** `endRental` (rules 4–5, then handoff / wait for a pending offer / wait for the next booking if `holdForNext` / collect), `scheduleEarlyReturn`, `extendRental`, delivery events (`onDeliveryPickedUp`, `onDeliveryDelivered` → `startTerm`), `recordCondition`, `closeItem`, `markCleaned`, `retireShoe`.
6. **Reservations (`reservations.ts`):** `cancelReservation` (payments' `releaseRent` + `unlockPairValue`, notify; `holdPair` → `holdForNext` on the holder), `handOffIfWaiting`, `acceptOffer`, `rejectOffer`, `expireOffers`.
7. **Notices (`src/server/notify`):** `notify(tx, …)` writes an in-app `Notification` inside the transaction; `flushEmails()` sends it through the `Mailer` adapter after commit (mock only; the provider is an open decision). Offers of new dates and price, cancelled reservations, rent locks lowered by someone else, and term ends are notified. Call `flushEmails` after every service that may notify.
8. **Condition reports:** grade A–D with photos at every handover. Damage can be flagged within 24 h. Grade D or w reaching L moves the shoe toward Retired.
9. **Close-out flow** (§5.4): when the receiver records condition, compute wear through `lastDay`, price it with **the shoe's own settings snapshot and pricing method** (`shoeCurve(shoe)`; never call `curveParams` directly, or an exponential-decay pair would be priced on the S-curve), with φ fixed from the shoe's state before this item (catch-up) and the item's discount, then round and split **merchant first** with `chargedAmounts`. Persist w_start, w_end, φ, the exact and charged amounts, update the shoe's wear, collectedSoFar, lostToDiscounts and merchantCollected, collect the rent (payments' `collectRent`: release the rent lock, charge the final rent), replan the pair, then let payments release the deposit (`settleItems`). Idempotent, in one DB transaction, so an item is never CLOSED (and its merchant share never payable) without its rent charged.
10. **Trips:** booking requests trips through logistics' `requestDelivery`, which sets the payer (the receiver pays the driver directly; the merchant for returns). Booking never charges, refunds or settles a trip fee, and a free (wrong-size) return doesn't change who pays the driver. Addresses come from the accounts, never from booking input: `customerAddress(tx, userId)` and `merchantAddress(tx, merchantId)` (`addresses.ts`), read when each trip is requested (DELIVER: merchant → customer; HANDOFF: giver → receiver; COLLECT: customer → merchant). `checkout` refuses a customer with no address ("Add your delivery address in your account before booking"), and `quoteBundle` marks a pair whose merchant has no address "not available".
11. **My rentals data (`queries.ts`):** term dates (`expected` dates for a reservation), planned wear, rent estimate locked (or the charge once closed), tokens locked, and the actions allowed now (`canEndNow`, `earlyReturn` date range, `canExtend`, offer, `keepingForNext` while an offer is pending).

## Must not

- Compute prices or shares yourself. Call the pricing engine.
- Price a listed shoe with the active settings row. Use its snapshot.
- Let a borrower end a term early without the notice period (except the free-return window).
- Hard-code the notice period, offer time, bundle size or release window. Read them from settings.
- Move money directly. Use payments (`lockRent`, `collectRent`, `releaseRent`, `unlockPairValue`, `finishRentalIfDone`) and the wallet.
- Charge rent before an item closes, or gate reservations on the release window.
- Collect a pair whose reservation was declined or expired after an early return: it waits with its holder (`holdForNext`) until the next booking.
- Talk to payment gateways, email providers or ride-hailing providers directly.
- Mark a rental closed before its condition report exists.
