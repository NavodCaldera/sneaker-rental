You build the data layer and core APIs for Nova, the shoe rental marketplace. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/pricing-spec.md` §1 ("Inputs") and `docs/business-model.md` §5, §6 and §8 first.

## Owns

- `prisma/**` (schema, migrations, `seed.ts`)
- `src/server/db.ts`, `src/server/auth/**`, `src/server/users/**`, `src/server/merchants/**`, `src/server/catalogue/**`, `src/server/settings/**`
- API route handlers for those areas under `src/app/api/{auth,users,merchants,shoes,settings}/**`

## Must deliver

0. **Demo data:** `scripts/demo-discounts.ts` (`npm run db:demo-discounts`, after `npm run db:seed`) makes realistic discounted pairs for the home page's "Exceptional Value" row by running real rentals through the services with past dates (checkout, deliveries, the daily job, `recordCondition`, `markCleaned`), after dating the seeded sample data back to a launch 60 days ago. Every shopping tab (Men, Women, Boys, Girls) must have at least one discounted pair (`RENTALS` in the script; unisex pairs count for Men and Women). Each customer's demo rentals are booked in date order. Never fake a discount by writing `releaseDay` or prices directly. Sample shoes have retail values between LKR 5,000 and 12,000 (product owner, 2026-10-13); new seed pairs stay in that range. The seed does not change shoes that already exist, so changing a sample value needs the dev rentals cleared and `npm run db:demo-discounts` run again (deposits are locked at V). Keep it working when booking or seed code changes (product owner, 2026-10-12).
1. **Prisma schema** for every entity in §8: User, Merchant, Shoe, PricingSettings, Rental, RentalItem, Delivery, ConditionReport, Payment, Payout, Dispute, Photo, Session.
   - Every money and wear field is `Decimal`, never `Float`: charged amounts `Decimal(14, 2)`, unrounded money and wear `Decimal(28, 12)`, ratios `Decimal(20, 16)`.
   - Enums: `Role` (MERCHANT, BORROWER, ADMIN), `ShoeStatus` (DRAFT, AVAILABLE, RESERVED, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, WITH_BORROWER, IN_HANDOFF, CLEANING, RETIRED, SOLD), `Grade` (A–D), `DeliveryType` (DELIVER, COLLECT, HANDOFF), plus status enums for Rental, RentalItem, Delivery, Payment, Payout, Dispute.
   - Shoe holds V, L, current wear w, `initialWear` (w₀ for a second-hand listing), `condition` (`ShoeCondition`: NEW, SECOND_HAND, SECOND_HAND_UPGRADED) and `upgradeNotes` (upgraded pairs only; null otherwise), **`pricingMethod`** (`PricingMethod`: LOGISTIC = S-curve, the default; EXPONENTIAL = exponential decay), collectedSoFar (charged on the platform, excluding F(w₀)), lostToDiscounts, latestGrade, and **`settingsId`: the settings version it is priced with**, set when created and refreshed when published. Changing settings affects new listings only.
   - RentalItem holds the delivery timestamps (`startDate` / `endDate`) and the inclusive wear-ledger days (`firstDay` / `lastDay`, Postgres DATE) that booking sets.
   - **Customer profile (product owner, 2026-10-13):** Nova is a **transformation economy**: each customer account belongs to one person, who at sign-up answers **"Whom do you want to become?"** in their own words, in a description box (10–500 characters): the personality they want to have and how people should see them, with our example as the box's placeholder, *"I want to build Sri Lanka's largest tech sensation"* (no "Other ideas" list: removed by the product owner, 2026-10-13) (product owner, 2026-10-13; it replaced five persona cards). Nova reads personas from the words (`personasFromStory`: The Athlete, The Professional, The Explorer, The Creative, The Icon, `PERSONAS` in `src/lib/borrower-profile.ts`, each mapped onto experience names, every experience covered; the example reads as Professional + Icon); customers never see persona names, their **gender** (Male / Female), **date of birth** (replaced the age in whole years; not in the future, at most 120 years) and **shoe size** (UK, EU or US label size, stored as "UK 9"). Stored on `User` (`becomingStory` their words, `becoming` the personas read from them, `gender`, `dateOfBirth` DATE, `shoeSize`); merchants and admins aren't asked. `readProfile` (`src/server/auth/service.ts`) checks it for sign-up and `updateProfile`; customers change it on `/account` ("About you", `ProfileForm`, shows their age). Shopping starts from it (`shoppingProfile`): under 13 → Boys or Girls, otherwise Men or Women by gender. **Browse** with no filters in the URL shows pairs in their size and for them ("Shoes in your size, UK 9 · Men, picked for who you want to become first. Show all shoes" → `/browse?all=1`; Clear and the "All" tab go there too), and always lists pairs made for their personas first (`rankForPersona`). **The Closet** starts from their size and audience when the URL and the draft cookie give none. The profile **never changes a price** (prices come only from the engine), except that the date of birth gives the birthday month's free rent (see "Birthday month"). Customers who signed up before it was asked see a prompt on `/account` and an unpersonalised Browse until they fill it in. No minimum age is enforced (open question); the `age` column was dropped (migration `20261013150000_borrower_profile`).
   - User holds `address` and `city` (both required at sign-up for customers and merchants: `addressSchema` 5–300 characters, `citySchema` 2–60; trips use `fullAddress` = "address, city"): the account's **one** address (customers and merchants; null for admins). Each customer and merchant account has **exactly one address**, required at sign-up with the reason shown next to the field (customers: where pairs are delivered and collected; merchants: where pairs are picked up and returned). It can be changed later on the account page (`/account`, `/merchant/account`); trips copy the addresses when they are booked, so a change applies to trips booked after it. There is no address at checkout or per listing (product owner, 2026-10-11). `Rental` and `Shoe` have no address columns (dropped in `20261011200000_account_address`, after copying the latest one to the account); `Delivery.fromAddress` / `toAddress` keep the addresses each trip used. `SessionUser` carries `address`.
   - PricingSettings is append-only (`effectiveFrom`): μm, μp, q, midpointRatio, baseSteepness, refLife (365), minLifespanDays / maxLifespanDays (180 / 730 = about 6–24 months), maxDiscount (0.10), releaseLeadDays (10: when the release discount starts; it no longer gates reservations), releaseDiscountStep (0.005), capitalRate r (0.12), holdCost h (0), catchUpEnabled, chargePastLife (true: wear past L is charged at P(L); part of each shoe's snapshot), offerResponseHours (24; open decision), earlyReturnNoticeDays (10), maxPairsPerBundle (4), disputeWindowDays (3) and freeReturnHours (24). There is no delivery or booking fee setting: trips are paid to the driver by the receiver.
   - Merchant holds `maxLifespanDays`, a longer maximum an admin granted on request (null = settings). Shoe holds `merchantCollected`, the merchant shares charged so far net of refunds (merchant first).
   - Shoe holds `releaseDay` (the current or last holder's term end; booking sets it) and `committedWear` (settled wear + open bookings' planned wear, kept by booking's `replan`; listings show it). Browse shows every available pair and every held pair nobody has reserved next, from the holder's day 1 (`browsableWhere`).
   - RentalItem holds the term and plan: `plannedDays`, `termEndDay`, `earlyReturnAt`, `plannedFirstDay`, `plannedWearStart` / `plannedWearEnd`, `plannedCharge` (rent estimate), `rentLocked` (tokens locked for it), `rentPaid` (rent charged: 0 until close), the offer (`offerFirstDay`, `offerCharge`, `offerWearStart`, `offerDueAt`), and `holdForNext` (a declined offer: the pair waits with this holder for the next booking). Rental holds `estimatedRent` and `shoeValue`.
   - Tokens: `WalletEntry` (append-only ledger: TOP_UP, WITHDRAWAL, LOCK, UNLOCK, CHARGE, CREDIT; positive amounts, unique idempotency key; locks are per pair with `rentalItemId` and a `purpose`, `LockPurpose` DEPOSIT or RENT), `Payment.userId` (gateway calls: TOP_UP, WITHDRAWAL with `refundOfId`).
   - Delivery: `paidBy` (`PartyPays`: BORROWER or MERCHANT) is the receiver who pays the driver directly; `quote` is the app's quoted trip fee, for information. No fee columns.
   - Notices: `Notification` (user, kind PRICE_OFFER / RESERVATION_CANCELLED / REFUND / TERM_ENDED, title, body, link, `readAt`, `emailedAt` as an email outbox).
   - Leave room for Phase 2 fields (borrower tier, lifespan accuracy score) without building their logic.
2. **Auth and roles:** sign-up/sign-in (sign-up requires the address and the city, `src/lib/address.ts`; `updateAddress(userId, address, city)` changes them), forgot password (`src/server/auth/password-reset.ts`: one-time emailed link, 60 minutes, one email a minute, hash-only `PasswordReset` rows; using it ends every session), role guards for merchant, borrower and admin, merchant verification flag and payout account. Courier access is a signed, expiring per-delivery link, not a user account.
3. **Catalogue APIs:** merchant CRUD for shoes (Draft → Available on publish), photos (front, side, sole, inside, box) through a storage adapter, and borrower search/filter by size in any system (UK, EU, US or cm: `sizeWhere` matches the `sizeUk`/`sizeEu`/`sizeUs`/`sizeCm` column; `shoeSizeFields` from `src/lib/shoe-size.ts` fills them when a shoe is created or its audience changes), brand and model (**optional**, product owner 2026-10-12: stored as "" when empty, shown through `shoeName()` as "Unbranded shoes"; `browseFacets().brands` leaves out ""), audience (a shopping tab: Men and Women include Unisex pairs) and **experiences** (`Shoe.experiences String[]`, product owner 2026-10-12: one or more, each from the audience's list, duplicates dropped; `styleSchema` / `shoeSchema`, lists in `src/lib/shoe-style.ts`; forms send repeated `experiences` fields, read with `formFields(formData)`; `browseShoes({ experience })` matches pairs whose list has it). User-facing messages say "days", never "wear-days" (e.g. "At least 180 days"). The listing schema takes its lifespan limits from `listingLimits(settings, merchant)` (6–24 months, or the merchant's admin-granted maximum) and accepts optional wear already used. Limits are checked when a shoe is created and published, never when it is priced. Admins grant or remove extensions with `setLifespanExtension` (in months). Listing responses include the shown price at the wear the next rental starts from (`nextWear` = `committedWear`), wear used, % of life left and the curve preview, **by calling `src/lib/pricing` through `shoeCurve` / `shoePricing`** (`src/server/catalogue/pricing.ts`) with the shoe's own settings snapshot. A new shoe starts with `committedWear = initialWear`.
   - **Stock per size (product owner, 2026-10-13):** a merchant lists a shoe once with the size system on its labels and **how many pairs they have of each size** (rows of size × pairs, `StockFields`; at most 20 pairs a size and 100 a listing, each size once). Every pair becomes its own `Shoe` row (its own size, wear, price, deposit and rentals, as before), all sharing a `listingId` and the same five photo files (one Photo row per pair). Publishing, deleting a draft, the pricing method and who it's for / experiences apply to the whole listing; retiring, cleaning and renting stay per pair. Borrowers see **one card per listing** (`groupByListing` / `cardPair` / `sizeRange` in `src/lib/listing.ts`): the pair with the biggest discount, then the lowest price, and the sizes on offer ("UK 7, 8, 9"; "From" when prices differ). The listing page has a **Sizes** picker (`listingSizes`): each size with how many pairs are free, or "reserve next"; picking one opens a pair of that size. The merchant's pair page has a "Pairs in this listing" card (`listingStock`). Sample listings declare `stock` in the seed (Dunk Low UK 8, 10 × 2, 11; Ultraboost Light EU 38 × 2, 39, 40; Revolution 7 Kids UK 12, 13 × 2).
   - **Listing condition (product owner, 2026-10-11):** merchants declare a pair as brand new, second-hand (no upgrades) or second-hand upgraded. `shoeSchema` enforces: condition required; NEW ⇒ wear used 0; second-hand (either kind) ⇒ wear used > 0; upgraded ⇒ `upgradeNotes` required (≤ 300 chars). `createShoe` stores `upgradeNotes` only for upgraded pairs. The condition is display information: never use it to price (the price comes from w₀ only). Labels live in `src/lib/shoe-condition.ts`.
   - **Pricing method (product owner, 2026-10-11; pricing-spec.md decision 11):** the merchant picks how a pair's price falls with wear: S-curve (logistic decay) or exponential decay. `shoeSchema` takes `pricingMethod` (`pricingMethodSchema`, defaults to `LOGISTIC`) and `createShoe` stores it. `updatePricingMethod(merchantId, shoeId, method)` changes it **only while the shoe is a DRAFT** (own shoes only; otherwise `CatalogueError` "The pricing method can only be changed before publishing"). Once published it is fixed, like `settingsId`, so rent estimates, reservations and the merchant's target never move under a rental. `shoeCurve` / `shoePricing` / `shoeCurvePreview` pass it to the engine; `curvePreview(V, L, settings, method)` and `GET /api/pricing/preview?…&method=` preview either curve. Every query that prices a shoe must load the scalar `pricingMethod` (it's on `PricedShoe`, required). The settings form keeps the floor q strictly between 0 and 1, since exponential decay needs it. Labels live in `src/lib/pricing-method.ts`.
4. **Seed data:** the acceptance-test shoes (6,000 LKR, L = 365 and L = 730), a few merchants and borrowers, and an admin, all listed under the seeded settings version. The A–E and hard-test scenarios are pure ledger tests and live in the unit tests, not the seed.

## Style packs

- **Style packs (product owner, 2026-10-13):** merchants and admins make curated rotations (`StylePack` → `StylePackRole` → `StylePackRoleShoe`; service `src/server/packs/`). A pack has a title, an optional subtitle and "Rotation advantage" line, and 1–8 roles; each role (name, optional description and experience) is filled by 1–4 **real listed pairs from any merchant**. A merchant's pack is a DRAFT until they send it for review (SUBMITTED); an **admin approves it before borrowers see it** (PUBLISHED) or rejects it with a reason (REJECTED, the merchant is notified with kind STYLE_PACK, edits and resubmits). A merchant editing a published pack sends it back for review. Admins can publish their own packs straight away, unpublish any pack (optional reason, merchant notified), and edit or delete any pack. Published packs show on the home page (`CuratedPacksShowcase`, newest first) and at `/packs/[id]`, where a borrower can add one bookable pair per role to the bundle ("Add the rotation to my bundle") or single pairs. Pairs that leave the catalogue drop out of a pack. **Packs carry no price or discount of their own**: each pair is rented at its own curve price, and the "about LKR x per day held together" line is `bundleDailyCost` of one pair per role (engine output). Manage at `/merchant/packs` and `/admin/packs` (nav "Style packs"). Validation (`packSchema` / `parsePackForm`, roles posted as JSON): title 3–80, subtitle ≤ 160, highlight ≤ 120, role name 2–60, description ≤ 160, experience from `ALL_EXPERIENCES` or empty, duplicate pairs dropped, every pair must be listed (not DRAFT/RETIRED/SOLD, verified merchant) or `PackError`. Only the maker or an admin can see, edit or delete a pack (`ownPack`).


## Birthday month

- **Birthday month (product owner, 2026-10-13; pricing-spec.md §8a):** in the calendar month of a customer's birthday no rent is charged for the wear on those days, for up to `birthdayFreePairs` (4) pairs held at once (the first ones they started; a fifth pays until one goes). The wear still counts; only the rent is waived. The ledger finds the days and wear (`birthdayWindows`, `birthdayFreeRanges`, `wearOverRanges`; `birthdayFreeWear` in `src/server/booking/plan.ts`) and the engine prices them (`incomeBetween(…, free)` → `freeCost`), in the plan (`plannedBirthdayFree`) and at close (`RentalItem.birthdayFree`). Applied after any discount, so nothing stacks. OPEN DECISION: platform-funded like the release discount (merchant target unchanged, merchant still paid first; waived rent goes to `lostToDiscounts`). The date of birth field says why we ask: "We value your celebrations: in your birthday month, rent is on us for up to 4 pairs. Under 13s are shown kids' shoes. Your date of birth stays private." (`birthdayReason`); the bundle, My rentals and (during the month) the account page show the saving. The "For example" line under "Whom do you want to become?" was removed (the example stays as the box's placeholder).

## Git

- **Every addition goes into `main` (product owner, 2026-10-11, restated 2026-10-13): no branch is ever left ahead of `main`.** After each commit (feature, fix, docs or rules), merge it into local `main` (`--no-ff` for a branch) and `git push origin main` in the same turn, after `npm run check` and `npm run test:int` (and `npm run e2e` when port 3100 is free). Work isn't done while it sits only on a branch, a worktree or an open PR. When another session's uncommitted edits in the shared checkout block the merge, set them aside (`git stash push --include-untracked`), merge and push, then put them back (`git stash apply`, resolving any overlap so both sides' work is kept, and drop the stash only once it's back), instead of waiting. Before finishing, `git branch -a --no-merged main` must list nothing but backup snapshots (e.g. `wip-uncommitted-*`, which are never merged).
