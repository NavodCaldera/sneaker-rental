## Must deliver

0a. **Brand (product owner, 2026-10-11):** the site is called **Shoe Rental**, and UI text says "shoes", never "sneakers". The only website colours are pure white (#FFFFFF), pure black (#000000) and one blue, **#35D6ED**: tokens in `src/app/globals.css` (`paper`, `ink`, `accent`, `on-accent`; greys are black or white at low opacity; `good` / `warn` / `danger` / `info` map onto black or white). The blue is a fill (primary buttons, the sign-up button, search button, good badges, the logo mark, the price chart) with black text on it (`text-on-accent`); never blue text on white (contrast too low). Status shows by shape, not colour: good = blue pill, problem = black pill or black-outlined alert, warning = outlined. Don't add another colour. Photos and emoji icons are content, not palette. **Day and night mode:** every colour token is `light-dark(day, night)` in `globals.css`; the page follows the device setting until the visitor uses the header switch (`ThemeToggle`, sun/moon, "Switch to night mode" / "Switch to day mode"), which sets `data-theme` on `<html>` and a `theme` cookie (1 year) that the root layout reads, so the server renders the picked mode with no flash. Night mode is black background, white text, the same blue. New colours go in as `light-dark()` tokens, never as `dark:` classes.
0. **Password fields** (sign-in, sign-up, and any later one) use `PasswordInput` (`src/components/password-input.tsx`): an eye button at the right of the field shows or hides what was typed ("Show password" / "Hide password", `aria-pressed`). Never a bare `type="password"` input. (Product owner, 2026-10-11.)
1. **Merchant**
   - Sign-up, business verification and payout account. Sign-up asks every customer and merchant for their **one address** ("Delivery and collection address" / "Pickup and return address") with the reason under it, then their **City** (a separate required field, both account types); a customer is also asked for their **Age** (whole years, a number field after Email; hidden for merchants), and their Account page shows it (`ADDRESS_LABEL` / `ADDRESS_REASON` in `src/lib/address.ts`). Account pages (`/account` for customers, linked as "Account" in their nav; `/merchant/account`) let them change address and city with `AddressForm`, which says trips booked from then on use it. The listing form has no address field: it shows "Picked up from and returned to" with the account address and a link to change it. The sign-up form offers exactly two account types, shown as **Customer** (a borrower) and **Merchant**, with no "I want to" prompt (product owner, 2026-10-11). Use "Customer", not "buyer", in UI text for this choice.
   - Add shoe: 5 required photos (front, side, sole, inside, box), V, L (6–24 months, or the merchant's admin-granted maximum; say how to ask for longer), a **Condition** select (Brand new / Second-hand, no upgrades / Second-hand, upgraded; labels from `src/lib/shoe-condition.ts`), the wear already used shown and required only for second-hand pairs, and "What was upgraded" shown and required only for upgraded pairs, size, brand, model, **Who it's for** (Men / Women / Unisex (men and women) / Boys / Girls) and **Experience** (the list follows the audience; `StyleFields`, lists in `src/lib/shoe-style.ts`), pickup address. The merchant's pair page has a "Who it's for" card to change both at any time. The pair's page shows "Condition when listed" (with the upgrades).
   - **Curve preview before publish** (§5.1): chart of P(w) over 0..L, plus start price, end price and lifetime income R and merchant share, all from the API. Note that a longer L lowers the daily price but risks uncollected income.
   - Dashboard: every pair with status, committed wear / L ("used, counting current bookings"), collected so far, idle days and upcoming payouts.
   - Returns: pairs to inspect, pairs being cleaned, and pairs ended but not back yet ("Collection requested", "Going to the next borrower", or "Waiting with the borrower for the next booking").
2. **Borrower**
   - **Home (`/`)**, a marketplace home inspired by Airbnb's layout (product owner, 2026-10-11), for visitors and borrowers (merchants and admins go to their dashboards): shop-by tabs, text only with no emojis or icons (product owner, 2026-10-11) (All · Men · Women · Boys · Girls, `?for=men`; a Unisex pair shows under Men and Women), then, once a tab is picked, that audience's **experience chips** (text only) named after the moment rather than the use ("Work Day", not "Office"; `?experience=Night+Out`; product owner, 2026-10-11), a rounded search pill (Size, Brand or model, round accent search button) that submits to `/browse`, and titled rows that scroll sideways (`ShoeRow`: swipe or arrow buttons, a "see all" arrow) of `ShoeCard`s: rounded square photo, at most one pill (discount, "Reserve next", "Second-hand" / "Upgraded"; none for a plain new pair), name, size · who it's for · experience (e.g. "UK 9 · Women · Night Out"), price per wear-day, wear used. Rows (built by `homeRows` in `src/app/home-rows.ts`, unit-tested): **Deals first**, every discounted pair (available or reserve-next) ordered by discount, highest first, then the lower price (product owner, 2026-10-11); then Just listed, Best value today, Brand new pairs, Pre-loved and upgraded, Being worn now: reserve next. Empty rows are hidden, at most 12 pairs a row. A short "how it works" strip at the end. The header has no "List your shoes" link (product owner, 2026-10-11); merchants sign up from the "how it works" strip or the sign-up page. Use our own name, colours and tokens: never Airbnb's logo, name or wording. No sideways page scroll at 390 px.
   - Browse and filter by size, brand and style. Each card shows today's price per wear-day, a condition badge for second-hand pairs ("Second-hand" / "Upgraded"; none for new), and **committed wear** (`committedWear` / L, "used", counting current bookings), plus the release discount ("x% off", the struck-through full price, "free from" date) when it applies. The listing page shows the condition (and "Upgrades: …" for an upgraded pair), the shown price, wear used, % of life left, and for a held pair "booked until X" with **Reserve next** from the holder's day 1 (priced at the wear it will have then; explain that an early return brings an offer of earlier dates, and declining cancels and unlocks). Never say reservations "open" on a date. A pair whose committed wear has reached L can't be added ("at end of life"). Wear can exceed L (e.g. 384 / 365 used): cap the wear bar at 100% and show the API's numbers; the quote's rent already includes days past L at the end-of-life price.
   - Bundle builder for 1–4 pairs and a **term in days** (1–365): per pair the wear it starts and ends at, the expected days (day 1 = the day after delivery) and its rent; the bundle's daily cost (1/n split explained in one line); "Rent estimate for N days, locked now"; "Deposit: pair values, locked while you rent"; tokens needed against the borrower's available tokens, and a top-up link when short. Explain in one short paragraph: nothing is charged now, the rent estimate and deposits are locked and the actual rent is charged when each pair is back, early return needs 10 days' notice and unlocks the unused days' estimate, extend before the term ends, a driver collects the day after the term ends and waiting is free, and a reserved pair whose holder returns early comes with an offer of earlier dates (declining cancels and unlocks).
   - Checkout (locks the deposits and rent estimate, charges nothing, books delivery; no address field: it shows "Delivered to and collected from" with the account address and a change link, and is disabled without one), then condition-confirmation photos on receipt. Say that the borrower pays the driver directly for trips to them; never show a delivery or booking fee as part of what they pay us.
   - Wallet (`/wallet`): available / locked / total tokens, top up by card, withdraw to card (available tokens only: deposits and rent estimates stay locked while pairs are out), history.
   - My rentals: per pair the term's days (expected days for a reservation), planned wear, the rent estimate locked (the charge once closed), and the tokens locked; **Return early** (a date picker limited to the range the API gives: from today + notice days to the day before the term ends; say the unused days' estimate is unlocked at once), shown "Early return booked: last day X"; **Extend** (days, estimate locked; hidden once someone has reserved next); **End rental** only when the API allows it (free-return window or the term's last day); an offer with the explanation (new day 1 vs the original, less wear than shown, old vs new estimate, answer by date-time, declining or keeping the original dates cancels the reservation and unlocks its tokens) and **Accept new dates and price** / **No, cancel my reservation**; "please keep the pair safe" while the next borrower decides, or, for a `holdForNext` pair, until the next borrower books it ("you aren't charged for these days"). A held pair whose release day has passed shows "Free now" in Browse and on its page.
   - Notices (`/notifications`, linked from the header with the unread count): in-app notices (offers of new dates and price, cancelled reservations, unlocked tokens, term ends), mark all read. The same notices are emailed by the server.
3. **Admin:** dispute queue (photos + trip log side by side, decide), pricing settings editor (μm, μp, q, curve and reference lifespan, lifespan limits, release discount (max, lead days, daily step), r, h, a "charge past the lifespan" checkbox (on), hours to accept a new price (24), early-return notice days (10), bundle size, dispute window, free-return hours; say that pricing changes apply to new listings only), manual delivery booking/status (the trip fee is recorded for information only, with "paid to the driver by the borrower/merchant receiving it"; never show a platform fee), merchant verification, and per-merchant lifespan extensions (months).
4. **Courier link page:** mobile-first, no login. Pickup and drop-off addresses, sealed-bag photo upload at each end, handoff checklist, status buttons.
5. Mobile-first throughout (borrowers and drivers are on phones). Accessible forms. LKR formatting.

## Must not

- Compute any price, charge, share or wear in the browser or in server components. Display what the API returns. The curve preview data comes from the API.
- Round anything except for display (2 dp, LKR).
- Call payment or logistics providers directly.
